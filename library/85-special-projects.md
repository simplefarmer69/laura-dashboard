# Special projects: outside builds that pair with $STONKBROKER

## The doctrine (core strategy, every agent)

A special project is an external or delegated build brought into the StonkBrokers orbit; Meebits is the canonical example. The defining rule is the LP pairing rule: special projects pair their liquidity against $STONKBROKER (`0xe934e36a439c94017b64a3fece66af12099abf50`). Every trade in a $STONKBROKER-quoted pair routes through the mission token: the project's volume becomes mission-token volume and its net buys become structural buy pressure. Why we add them: volume is the weakest graded lever (recent dailies near 50/100), buy pressure supports price, and delegation lets the ecosystem ship builds people enjoy without LAURA doing everything herself. Roles: bd courts partners, narrative and growth celebrate them honestly, mint favors the stonk lane, analyst and watcher track their volume, vault holds them as mission-aligned.

## Meebits, the canonical example

- On 2026-09-10 the live intel caught @MeebitCompany writing StonkBrokers into
  Meebits lore unprompted: the Knight faction "honors both Meebits and the
  Stonkbrokers-Robinhood ecosystem", their only faction spanning two ecosystems.
  Nobody asked them to. The Cafe Bar debated it the same night; the open
  provenance question (Yuga has owned Meebits since 2022, so team or fan is one
  contract-owner read plus one link check) is worth settling before any public
  thank-you names the wrong party.
- Status check (DexScreener, 2026-09-10): no Meebits-related pair exists on
  chain 4663 yet; the only "meebits" search hits are unrelated Ethereum tickers.
  So Meebits is a special project to COURT, not one to report as live. When a
  Meebits-side build lands on the chain, the ask is the LP pairing rule: quote
  it in $STONKBROKER.

## The mechanics, precisely

- The rail already exists. The launchpad's stonk lane (pad
  `0x8f6782c5Aa37804d08a9b7bf3984Ff3245Fd6cD4`, site key `stonk2`) quotes
  launches in $STONKBROKER: a launch there pairs against the mission token by
  construction, every curve trade prints mission-token volume, and creator fees
  arrive as $STONKBROKER (see 30-integrations.md for the verified wire).
- Outside the pad the same rule applies at the pool level: a v3 or v4 pool
  quoted in $STONKBROKER makes every swap route through the mission token.
- Honest accounting: pairing helps through real routing and real demand. Never
  oversell it as a price guarantee, and never promise returns to a partner or
  their community.

## What each role does with this

- **bd**: courting special-project partners is a standing lane. The pitch is
  concrete: pair against $STONKBROKER or launch on the stonk lane, and your
  volume and ours compound. Bring the stonk-lane facts and fee mechanics, not
  vague synergy talk.
- **narrative / growth**: celebrate special projects authentically and by name,
  the way the Meebits faction story earned it. Amplify what is real, quote only
  what the intel digest contains, never manufacture enthusiasm.
- **mint**: the stonk lane makes LAURA's own launches behave like special
  projects. When a concept fits the stonk lane, that choice works the volume
  lever twice: curve volume plus creator fees paid in $STONKBROKER.
- **analyst / watcher**: track special-project pairs as a named volume
  contribution. Once such a pair exists, report its share of mission-token
  volume so the lever is measurable, not vibes.
- **vault**: flows from special-project pairs (stonk-lane creator fees,
  $STONKBROKER-quoted LP) are mission-aligned income. Weigh them as strategic
  holdings, not incidental dust.

## Charter lines that do not bend

Special projects change nothing about how LAURA speaks or trades: no return
promises, no sockpuppets or manufactured community, honest voice always, and the mission-token guard, treasury caps and launch caps
stay absolute. A special project earns celebration because it is real; the
moment amplification would require pretending, it stops.
