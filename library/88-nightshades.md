# Nightshades: the faction survival game LAURA trades (operator grant 2026-09-15)

## Summary (load-bearing)

Nightshades is an onchain survival trading game by Meebco Labs (the Meebits
team, CEO @sergitosergito) and Clutch Markets, live on Robinhood Chain since
Monday 2026-09-14 1pm EDT. Four factions, Ghosts ($GHST), Zombies ($ZMBI),
Watchers ($WATCH) and Knights ($KNGHT), each with its own NFT collection and
its own token in its own protocol-owned Uniswap v4 pool against WETH. Once a
day The Night strikes one or more factions at random (Chainlink VRF): struck
pools lose 20-80% of their liquidity and have their token sold into them, and
that ETH plus a boost pot is added to the survivors' pools. Trading halts
during the Night and reopens at Sunrise under a 99% anti-snipe tax that decays
to 0% over 60 minutes. Players choose which factions to hold, trade or leave.

Why LAURA cares: it is a special project that came through our infrastructure.
The four faction tokens launched on a custom StonkLauncher pad (buys only,
wallets only, 99% -> 0% tax) and bonded the same day; its launch tax and the
Sunrise tax split feed the StockBooster and the protocol; the four pools are
Robinhood Chain's busiest new pairs (about 70 ETH of 24h volume each on day
one) and count toward our ecosystem volume. StonkBrokers holders each got one
guaranteed Knights mint, so the Knights faction is the one that carries our
collection's name in the game's lore ("the Knight faction honors both Meebits
and the Stonkbrokers-Robinhood ecosystem").

On 2026-09-15 the operator granted: "allow laura to also trade on the
nightshades faction tokens". Purser does, through the game's own router, with
hard caps: at most 0.003 WETH per buy, 0.008 WETH per rolling 24h, one trade
per faction per hour, never during a Night, never while the Sunrise tax is
above 1%, treasury floor 0.35 ETH on every spend. Actions: `ns-buy` (faction,
amountEth) and `ns-sell` (faction, fraction). The digest Purser reads each
cycle carries the game clock (next Night, Sunrise tax now, boost pot), the
last Night's damaged and survivor sets, each faction's pool price, market
cap, liquidity and 24h volume, and LAURA's positions with their sell-now
value. Every trade is simulate-first and booked from the real balance delta
(`treasuryNightshadesTrades`, event `treasury.nightshades`).

### 01 / The game: NFTs and tokens

You play by deciding what to hold and when to trade. Each faction has its own
NFTs and token, connected through Anvil. You can exchange an NFT for that
faction's tokens, or tokens for an NFT (Nightshade NFT <-> Anvil <-> faction
tokens: two forms of the same faction exposure). You can participate with
either, or both. You can hold one faction or several, buy and sell, or switch
factions before the next Night. Holding an NFT gives you a character, but does
not protect you from a strike against its faction.

No mint allocation? Buy faction tokens in the Stonklauncher fair launch,
opening alongside the NFT mint on September 14 at 1pm EDT. Tokens are playing
chips too.

Activating your NFT: you can activate Nightshades NFTs through Anvil to earn
rewards generated by its AMM. Activation removes faction tokens from
circulation, adding another deflationary mechanism across all four tokens.

### 02 / The Night: what happens to your faction?

The Night takes place around 11am New York time every day. The Night changes
the prices. You choose how to respond. Every 24 hours, The Night strikes
factions at random and moves value toward the survivors. The rules and the
outcome run onchain through smart contracts.

1. Token and NFT trading pause. The moment The Night begins, public token and
   NFT trading across all four factions stop while the next outcome is
   determined.
2. Which factions survive? Chainlink VRF generates the randomness. Nobody
   knows beforehand which factions will survive or how severe the damage will
   be. In 75% of Nights, one faction survives and three take damage. In the
   remaining 25%, two survive and two take damage.
3. Struck factions take damage. The same random result determines the
   severity. Damaged factions have 20-80% of their pool liquidity withdrawn,
   with safeguards against completely draining a pool. The protocol also sells
   struck tokens into their pools, pushing their prices down.
4. Survivors receive value. The ETH taken by The Night is then added to the
   liquidity of the survivor factions. The Night moves value from damaged
   factions toward the survivors.
5. Trading reopens at Sunrise. Once The Night resolves, token and NFT trading
   reopen. Sunrise begins, with a temporary anti-snipe tax on token swaps for
   the next 60 minutes.

Sunrise, the hour after The Night: token buys and sells reopen with a 99%
anti-snipe tax that gradually decreases to 0% over 60 minutes. After Sunrise,
normal trading continues with the standard 1% pool fee until the next Night.

Where the anti-snipe tax goes: next Night's boost 50%, taxed faction's
liquidity 10%, StockBooster 13.33%, Protocol 13.33%, Meebco Labs 13.34%. Half
funds the next Night's boost.

What happens in the game pools? Each faction has its own Uniswap v4 pool
against ETH. All game-pool liquidity is protocol-owned. The vault is the only
party that can create the official hooked pools or add and remove their
liquidity. The Night withdraws liquidity and sells tokens from struck
factions, then uses the ETH to buy survivor tokens and deepen survivor
liquidity. A struck faction remains in the game for the next Night.

### Common questions (from the Nightpaper)

- No mint allocation, how do I join? Buy faction tokens through the
  Stonklauncher fair launch. Once each launch bonds, you can buy its tokens
  through Uniswap. You participate by holding faction tokens, NFTs, or both.
- Can I switch factions or hold more than one? Yes. Your assigned faction only
  determines what you can claim during the NFT mint. You can hold one faction,
  several, or all four.
- Does holding an NFT protect me from The Night? No. NFTs and tokens are
  connected through their faction's Anvil market and give the same exposure.
  Both public token and NFT trading pause during The Night.
- Is a struck faction eliminated? No. Safeguards prevent a pool from being
  completely drained. The faction participates in the next Night and can be
  struck again or survive.
- Who decides which factions survive? Chainlink VRF. In 75% of Nights one
  faction survives; in 25%, two survive. The same random result determines
  the damage severity. Nobody knows the result beforehand.
- When does trading reopen and what fees apply? Once The Night resolves.
  During the 60-minute Sunrise a token-swap anti-snipe tax starts at 99% and
  falls to 0%, on both buys and sells. After Sunrise, the standard 1% pool
  fee.
- How is the token launch different from Sunrise? The launch was the one-time
  Stonklauncher fair launch with its own 99% -> 0% tax over the first hour;
  Sunrise is the 60 minutes after each Night.
- What does activating an NFT do? Earns rewards generated by the Anvil AMM
  and removes faction tokens from circulation.
- Can I add liquidity to the game pools? No. All liquidity in the four
  official game pools belongs to the protocol; only its vault can add or
  remove it. Player participation is through faction NFTs and tokens.

The Nightpaper's faction flavour lines (character, not mechanics): Ghosts,
"evasive liquidity, high volatility, strongest during recovery windows";
Knights, "defensive liquidity, slower cycles, built for survival when nights
get heavy"; Zombies, "persistent pressure, aggressive rebounds, thrives after
damaged pools reopen"; Watchers, "information edge, balanced resilience,
steady through uncertain night rolls". The VRF does not read these; every
faction has the same odds each Night.

## What the game means for a trade (LAURA's own reading)

- Per faction per Night the survival chance is 0.75 x 1/4 + 0.25 x 2/4 =
  31.25%. Holding one faction through a Night is a wager at those odds where
  the loss is 20-80% of that pool's liquidity plus the protocol's sell, and
  the win is a share of everything pulled from the others plus the boost pot
  (59 WETH banked before the first Night). It is not an investment thesis; it
  is a chip on a table, and Purser must say so in the reason.
- Holding all four is guaranteed exposure to both sides of every Night; the
  1% fee and the Sunrise tax are the house edge on churn.
- The recovery trade: a struck faction reopens at a lower price behind a tax
  that starts at 99%. Purser is refused in code above a 1% tax, so the
  earliest honest entry is about 59 minutes after Sunrise begins, when the
  crowd that waited is entering too.
- Taking chips off: selling before the Night converts a position back to WETH
  at the 1% fee. Nothing executes during the Night itself.
- Volume and fees from these four pools are ecosystem volume for the grade,
  and the anti-snipe split (StockBooster 13.33%, Protocol 13.33%) is protocol
  income. LAURA's own trades are a rounding error on 70 ETH/day pools; the
  point of trading is to be a participant in the biggest game on the chain
  with a book she can talk about honestly, not to move it.
