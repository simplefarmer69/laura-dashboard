# Cross-agent dedupe

Every critic veto in the record (narrative x4, growth x3 as of 2026-09-10) was for repetition, and three of them hinged on a rationale that claimed novelty falsely because the agent checked only its own titles or left out the draft it copied. Each veto costs about 1.3 execution points (~0.2 grade points) — the same as a whole protocol-volume point. This procedure is how a producer earns a pass.

## Before drafting

1. List every title from ALL agents (narrative, steward, bd, analyst, researcher, growth, mint) in the last two cycles. Own titles are not enough; the critic reads the whole swarm.
2. Name the single closest earlier draft. If you cannot name one, you have not looked.
3. State in one sentence what your draft changes relative to it: audience, channel, format, or new data with its source (DexScreener, DefiLlama, RPC, docs page). 'Same mechanic, new hook' is not a change.
4. Never write 'a surface no agent has touched' or 'first time covered' unless the list confirms it.

## Spine rule

- The spine (the mechanic or surface in post 1 / paragraph 1 / the outreach ask) may not be the spine of any draft on the two-cycle list.
- 666,666 per Anvil swap, the 70/30 ETH fee split, the 50% activation burn and borrow-vs-sell are supporting facts only; they never open a draft and never fill a gap when the chosen surface lacks documented mechanics.
- A surface qualifies as a spine only if the docs excerpt or library in your prompt states at least one mechanic about it. If it does not, do not write a thread that says the page exists — route the surface to researcher and pick another spine.

## Context block

- The day's fee / revenue / price trio belongs to the analyst daily report and the steward floor report only. Every other draft cites at most one such figure, once, with its source.
- 'Sink data not readable today' is one clause, not a paragraph re-listing the sinks.

## Recurring formats

- Intentionally recurring formats (analyst daily report, steward floor report, bd rotating outreach) are exempt from the spine rule but not from the title rule: one report per UTC day, dates pinned to TODAY (UTC), later cycles produce delta notes only.

## In the rationale

Paste the two-cycle title list, name the closest draft, state the change. A rationale without these three items is a repeat waiting for a veto. If the honest answer is 'nothing new this cycle', skip and say why — a skipped draft costs nothing.
