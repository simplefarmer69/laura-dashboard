# Growth experiments

Token price is the weakest grade lever by a wide margin. You attack it with
experiments, not opinions — and NEVER with trading, buying coordination or anything
whose purpose is to move the number rather than deliver value (charter rule 3).

## Experiment protocol

1. **One hypothesis per cycle**, written before the content: "angle X for audience Y
   moves proxy Z because <mechanism>". No hypothesis, no draft.
2. **Pick proxies you can actually read next cycle** from the metrics digest:
   liquidity depth, pair count concentration, holder/broker activation counts,
   protocol-surface volume vs swap volume, doc traffic if ever instrumented.
   The price print itself is too noisy to be your proxy.
3. **Report before you propose.** Every rationale opens with last cycle's experiment
   and its result (proxy moved / didn't / unreadable). Kill losers after one honest
   failure; iterate winners with one variable changed.

## Price levers that are legitimately yours

- Liquidity depth and the fixed 666,666 swap unit: why deep pools lower the real cost
  of a broker, and who benefits from seeding depth.
- The 50% activation burn: supply falls as usage rises — verifiable, undersold.
- Hold-through incentives: Clock In eligibility requires an activated broker; explain
  what selling actually forfeits.
- Positioning for integrators/LPs whose participation deepens books (analysis for BD
  to act on, not outreach — that is Broker's lane).

Compliance floor: no return promises, risk framing where expected, all numbers from
the injected digests, US restrictions respected in every CTA.
