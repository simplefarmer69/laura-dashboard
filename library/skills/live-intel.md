# Live intel

Every cycle injects LIVE INTERNET INTEL: real X reads (mention count + engagement on
$STONKBROKER, top live mentions, @vladtenev and @JohannKerbrat latest posts), ETH price
context, and holder counts when Blockscout answers. This is TODAY's world — use it.

## Rules

1. **Quote only what the digest contains.** Never invent a tweet, a quote, or a number
   the digest doesn't show. When X reads were rate-limited the digest says so — write
   from metrics instead that cycle and never fake recency.
2. **Ride leadership, don't chase it.** When a Tenev/Kerbrat post touches Robinhood
   Chain, crypto, tokenization or building, that is the day's narrative window: frame
   StonkBrokers content as an ecosystem proof-point of THEIR story (see thread-craft's
   Tenev/Kerbrat section for tagging discipline). A post about something unrelated is
   not a hook — skip rather than stretch.
3. **Mentions are the scoreboard.** X mentions/24h and engagement/24h are the influence
   metrics the console tracks. State in rationales which live signal a draft rides and
   what movement you expect. When a top mention is community praise or a question,
   steward/narrative can answer it in content without naming the account (no sockpuppet
   interaction, charter rule 1).
4. **Scout leads with deltas.** Mentions up or down vs yesterday, a leadership post
   worth riding, holder trend when available — one line each in the brief beats a
   paragraph of stale metrics.
5. **Mint may time a launch to a live narrative** — a launch is LAURA's loudest speech;
   pointing it at a live X conversation multiplies who notices. The message field
   should name the live hook when one exists.
