# Output budget

Draft bodies are stored in a fixed-size field. On 2026-09-10 four drafts were cut off mid-sentence and every one was vetoed: narrative 'Build here' (post 8), 'Zero findings' (post 8, ending 'and non'), 'Two token lists' (ending 'Bloc' inside the sink clause) and growth Exp 06 (post 3). In three of the four the lost text was the sink or risk-framing close, so the shipped body lacked the framing rule 2 of the charter requires. The same overflow is the likely source of the schema repairs in the run log (1, 1, 2 in the last three runs).

The exact field limit is not published to agents. The working budget below is conservative and is the standard until the operator states the real limit.

## Rules

- Keep the total body (thread plus article plus any appendix) under about 4,500 characters and the rationale under about 1,200 characters. Write to the LOWER end of any word range in your strategy when the spine is thin.
- Front-load what must survive. Risk framing (fee-funded smart-contract mechanics, not dividends or equity; stock-token features unavailable in the United States where relevant) and the source for any number appear no later than post 3 of a thread and in the first half of an article. The CTA goes last because it is the safest thing to lose.
- One complete format beats two padded ones. If the material supports a thread but not an article, ship the thread and say so in the rationale.
- Never exceed a per-post cap (narrative: 260 chars) by stuffing; split into another post or drop the sentence.
- Before submitting, read your own last line. If it does not end with a full stop and the CTA, the body is too long: cut a middle post or paragraph, never the risk framing or the source citation.
- A body cut mid-word is a defect the critic vetoes and costs about 1.3 execution points. A shorter, complete draft passes.
- Rationale content: the two-cycle title list from the cross-agent-dedupe skill belongs in the rationale, so budget for it; list titles only, not summaries.
