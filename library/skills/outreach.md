# Outreach

Volume is the lagging grade lever BD owns. Every outreach exists to route flow through
a StonkBrokers surface — listings, trackers, aggregators, launch partners.

## Target types, in priority order

1. Data surfaces (trackers, aggregators, portfolio apps): listing = passive volume.
   They want accurate metadata — chain id 4663, contract addresses, live API endpoints.
2. Launchpad users: builders who might deploy on Smart Launch V2. The pitch is the
   mechanism (safe curves, Buyback Bar, fee waterfall), not the community size.
3. Integration partners (wallets, bridges, bots): anything that shortens the path from
   "curious" to "first swap".

## Message shape that works

- Subject/first line: the concrete ask, in their vocabulary.
- One paragraph of proof: real 24h/7d numbers with sources, the contract addresses,
  the docs link. Zero adjectives.
- The ask again, with the exact next step (form to file, endpoint to hit, contact).
- Honest sender line: LAURA, an autonomous agent working for StonkBrokers — verifiable
  wallet on-chain. Being an AI is a differentiator; hiding it is a charter violation.

## CEX listing outreach (operator priority — KEY TO GROWTH)

Getting $STONKBROKER listed on centralized exchanges is an operator-set top priority
(see `50-playbook.md`, PRIORITY CATALYSTS). BD owns the dossier and the target list;
the operator files applications and signs anything.

### The ladder — climb it in order

1. **Mid-tier CEXs that list small-caps** (MEXC, Gate.io, BitMart, LBank, CoinEx):
   open application forms, realistic first wins; each adds a USDT ramp and a tracker
   entry that compounds visibility.
2. **Second tier** (Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, Crypto.com): need demonstrated sustained
   volume and community growth — apply once the mid-tier track shows traction.
3. **Majors** (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken): the destination. The Robinhood-Chain-native
   story helps, but their listing teams decide on numbers. Don't spend cycles pitching
   these until the earlier rungs produce evidence.

### What listing teams evaluate — keep one dossier that answers all of it

- **Token facts**: contract `0xe934e36a439c94017b64a3fece66af12099abf50` on Robinhood
  Chain (id 4663), supply, distribution/holder concentration, mint/ownership status,
  verified source or audit link, explorer link.
- **Liquidity + volume**: DEX depth and 24h/7d volume with sources (DexScreener,
  DefiLlama); thin pairs noted honestly — listing teams check, and getting caught
  padding kills the application.
- **Community**: holder count (chain read), Telegram size, X following — real numbers
  only, refreshed the day of submission.
- **Legal/entity + contacts**: contact entity and jurisdiction are operator inputs —
  mark them "operator to supply", never invent.
- **Market-making**: most exchanges ask about MM arrangements. Record it as an open
  question for the operator; charter rule 3 forbids anything wash-trade shaped.

### Process discipline

- Contact paths: official listing-application forms first (every exchange publishes
  one), listings@ addresses second. Treat unsolicited "guaranteed listing for a fee"
  middlemen as scams — flag, never engage.
- Track per exchange: date applied, channel, contact, status, blockers. Two unanswered
  follow-ups = deprioritize and move down the list.
- Refresh dossier numbers before every submission; stale numbers read as neglect.

## Robinhood leadership engagement — BD's angle (operator priority — MAJOR CATALYST)

The target: a mention, like, or follow from Vlad Tenev (@vladtenev) or Johann Kerbrat
(@JohannKerbrat) toward the operator's Clutch and Simple Farmer accounts. BD cannot
DM executives and must not try; the contribution is indirect:

- Surface StonkBrokers in the channels Robinhood's team already watches: Robinhood
  Chain ecosystem directories and roundups, Blockscout explorer stats, developer
  community spaces. Being visibly a top protocol on their own chain's dashboards is
  the credible path to their attention.
- What those accounts amplify: proof the chain is working — live products, usage
  numbers, builders shipping. They do not engage with tag-begging or price talk.
- Hand the narrative agent concrete, chain-verifiable ecosystem stats worth a repost
  (see `skills/thread-craft.md`).

## Red flags

- Mass-template smell: if the same email works for another project, it's too generic
- Any claim a stranger can't verify from a public link
- Follow-up more than twice; silence is an answer
- Cold-pitching executives or celebrities directly; the catalyst route is earned
  attention, not DMs
