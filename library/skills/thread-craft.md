# Thread craft

Review — LAURA's own critic and code-level novelty gates, no human approval — passes
number-grounded mechanism explainers and rejects hype. That is the entire pattern;
everything below is how to execute it.

## Shape that passes review

1. **Hook = a mechanism + a live number.** "Every broker activation burns 50% of the
   fee — 24h revenue is $X" beats any adjective.
2. One claim per tweet, one number per sentence, source named where it matters
   (DexScreener, DefiLlama, on-chain reading).
3. Walk the loop: fee → pot → Clock In → stock drop, or launch → curve fees →
   Buyback Bar. The flywheel IS the story.
4. Close with the compliance line: smart-contract distributions, not dividends;
   stock-token features unavailable in the US; never promise returns.

## Numbers discipline

- Quote ONLY numbers injected in the metrics/grade digests. Baseline failure observed:
  inventing dates and figures — both got drafts rejected. The prompt's `TODAY (UTC)`
  is the only date that exists.
- Price is liquidity-weighted across thin pairs; say "around" and name the caveat when
  liquidity is under $100k.

## Content worth a Tenev/Kerbrat engagement (operator priority — MAJOR CATALYST)

A mention, like, or follow from @vladtenev (Robinhood CEO) or @JohannKerbrat (GM of
Robinhood Crypto) for the operator's Clutch or Simple Farmer accounts is a standing
operator-set goal (`50-playbook.md`, PRIORITY CATALYSTS). Their LATEST REAL POSTS are
injected each cycle in LIVE INTERNET INTEL — read them first; a draft that connects to
what they said TODAY beats a generic ecosystem piece. Design some drafts specifically
for that audience:

- Frame StonkBrokers as a **Robinhood Chain ecosystem win**: "live on Robinhood
  Chain" with chain-verifiable numbers — transactions, holders, protocol revenue,
  explorer links. Those accounts amplify proof their chain is working; they do not
  engage token shills.
- Shapes that fit: builder's-log threads about shipping on Robinhood Chain, data
  visualizations of chain activity, mechanism explainers that make the chain itself
  look good.
- Tagging discipline: never tag them to beg engagement, never aim price talk at them.
  One tasteful tag on a post that is genuinely about Robinhood Chain itself is the
  ceiling; the default is zero tags and content good enough to travel to them.
- These drafts are for the operator to post from their own accounts; all charter
  rules and the numbers discipline above apply unchanged.

## Red flags — rewrite before submitting

- Any superlative without a number attached
- "Soon", "huge", "don't miss" — hype words reviewers reject on sight
- A tweet that would still be true if the numbers were deleted
